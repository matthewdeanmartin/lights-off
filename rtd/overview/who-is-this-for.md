# Who is Lights Off for?

## Primary audience

**Blind and low-vision Mastodon users** who use NVDA, JAWS, or VoiceOver as their primary
means of interacting with a computer. The application was designed so that a user who never
looks at the screen can still browse timelines, post, reply, boost, and manage their account
entirely by keyboard and ear.

## Secondary audience

**Power users who prefer keyboard-only workflows.** If you are sighted but would rather not
reach for the mouse, Lights Off's accelerator-heavy interface may appeal to you.

## What you need

- A Mastodon account on any instance (e.g. `mastodon.social`, `fosstodon.org`, etc.)
- Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+
- Python 3.12 or newer (if installing from PyPI)
- A working screen reader (optional but the main use case)

## What you do not need

- Any familiarity with the command line beyond a single `pip install` step
- A Twitter or X account — Mastodon is a fully separate, open network
